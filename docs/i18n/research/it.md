## Decisions at a glance

- **hreflang:** `it` (not `it-IT`), with `x-default` set to English. Autocomplete for the main seeds is identical for gl=it, gl=ch and gl=sm, and no regional vocabulary differs. `<html lang="it">`.
- **URLs:** `/it/<italian-slug>`, home at `/it` (no trailing slash, per `normalizeCanonical`). Each page has a self-referencing canonical, and en↔it alternates appear only where an Italian page exists.
- **Ship 12 pages** (home, 6 tools, 4 guides, plus audio as low priority) and **skip 7** for lack of demand, a mismatched intent, or an honesty problem.

## Typography

Big Shoulders Display latin subset (800/900) has every Italian glyph (À È É Ì Ò Ù à è é ì ò ù ’ « » € checked with fontkitten). Uppercase IT H1s measure −33% to +5% of the EN H1 width at weight 900; the longest word is 'SCARICARE' (≈180px at 46px), so there is no overflow risk at 343px. Risk: accented capitals rise to 0.98em against a 0.80em cap height, so at line-height 0.88–0.95 an È/À/Ù on a wrapped second line can touch the line above. The proposed H1s contain no accented letters. For H2s like 'È GRATIS?', use line-height ≥1.0 under :lang(it) or rephrase ('PintDownload è gratis?' still uppercases È mid-line, which is harmless on the first line). Always write 'È', never "E'". Italian body copy runs ~10–20% longer than English, so check the stub/collect button labels ('SCARICA TUTTO', 'PROVA UN ALTRO LINK') at 320px.

## Risks

- The downloader UI and API errors are English-only (DownloaderForm: 'Admit one · public link', 'Void', 'Try another link'; /api/extract: 'Invalid link…'). An Italian landing page with an English ticket and English errors weakens the page, so localize the form strings first.
- Cannibalisation pairs: scaricare-video-da-pinterest vs come-scaricare-video-da-pinterest vs convertitore-pinterest-mp4, and come-salvare-video-pinterest-in-galleria vs scaricare-video-pinterest-iphone-android. Each has a distinct primary keyword above. Watch GSC and merge if two URLs swap for one query.
- Aranzulla.it dominates Italian 'come …' queries with long editorial guides, so the how-to pages need real depth (per-device steps with the Italian Pinterest labels) or they won’t rank.
- 'senza filigrana' is the top modifier. Claim only that we add no watermark; don’t promise removing burned-in watermarks.
- The pinterest.it and it.pinterest.com URLs Italians paste are accepted by the extract regex (it. subdomain and pinterest.[a-z.]+).
- Legal FAQs ('è legale'): give an honest, non-lawyer answer (personal use; copyright stays with the creator; ask permission to republish) and no guarantees.
